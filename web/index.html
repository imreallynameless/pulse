Before you connect

  1. Wear your Air and keep it near your Mac.
  2. In Google Health, open Connections → Fitbit Air → Share heart rate → Get started.
  3. Use Chrome, then select your tracker in the Bluetooth picker. Allow Chrome Bluetooth access if macOS asks.

If your tracker is missing, enable sharing again and disconnect other apps using its heart-rate feed.

Use this only if the normal heart-rate filter cannot find your Air.
