Pulse privacy policy

Effective October 28, 2026. This policy describes the personal Pulse deployment operated by Lei Wu at pulse-heart-rate.leiwuhoo.workers.dev. Independently hosted forks have their own operators.

Google Health access

The owner authorizes read-only access to Google Health metrics and measurements. Pulse requests heart-rate data and checks that the Google Health connection is available. It stores the latest valid BPM and its original measurement time, not a history of heart-rate measurements.

When the owner turns on the optional health page, Pulse also requests read-only activity and sleep access and stores one daily value per enabled metric for up to 365 days: steps, active zone minutes, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, breathing rate, skin temperature change, VO2 max, the last weight logged each day, and sleep duration with stage minutes. This deployment enables them for laywu.com/health.

What is public

Connecting the account starts public sharing of its latest BPM and measurement timestamp. Anyone with the feed or embedded widget address can view or copy them. Public responses also include update status and the server time. With the health page on, the enabled daily values, their sync status and last update time are public at /health.json too. Public responses do not expose Google tokens, account identity, device details or individual measurements behind the daily values.

Storage and providers

Google access and refresh tokens are encrypted before being stored in a Cloudflare Durable Object. Client credentials and the encryption key are held in Cloudflare Worker secrets. Cloudflare hosts the service and processes requests, including ordinary connection information such as IP addresses. Google processes authorization and Health API requests. These providers' own policies apply to their services.

Pulse uses secure cookies for owner sessions and sign-in state. It does not add advertising or analytics trackers. Application request logging is disabled. Hosting providers may retain operational or security records under their own policies.

Use and retention

Google Health data is used only to provide the owner-requested heart-rate widget and health page. Pulse does not sell this data or use it for advertising or AI training. The latest reading is replaced when a newer measurement arrives. Daily values older than 365 days are deleted. Tokens, the latest reading and daily values remain stored across restarts until the owner disconnects; a failed refresh can leave the old reading visible with its age and a paused status.

Stopping sharing

On the owner connection page, choose “stop sharing & disconnect” to delete the stored authorization, latest reading and daily values and stop serving them publicly. This does not revoke Google's app grant; remove Pulse from your Google account's third-party connections to revoke it separately. Deleting data here cannot retract copies already viewed or saved by others.
